Robot Operator Console

2026

Sitting at the console of a working robot. A humanoid runs a warehouse pick-and-place loop while the control interface reads its vitals: battery, joint load, gait stability, alarms that wait to be acknowledged. Watch from above, through its eyes, or in VR. Made with Unity MCP and Claude Code.

Solo project built in Unity, exploring what it feels like to sit at the console of a working robot. A humanoid runs a warehouse pick-and-place loop: approach, grasp, lift, carry, place, return. An industrial control interface reads its vitals: battery, joint load, gait stability, alarms that flash red and wait to be acknowledged. Watch from above, through the robot's own eyes, or free-flying in VR. Made with Unity MCP and Claude Code.

Retune by measurement, not by eye: `scale = winW / naturalW`, `over = naturalH*scale - winH`, visible source band = `[over*p/100/scale, that + winH/scale]`, then read the target element's y-range straight off the capture (a colour scan for the gate's teal ring works well).
